FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which operators are supported?
Calendar Wala currently works with Robi (016) and Airtel (018) numbers. Other operators will be added in the future.
How much does it cost?
Subscription: 2.78 tk/day including VAT + SD + SC for Robi and Airtel subscribers. The charge is added to your mobile bill and you can unsubscribe at any time from inside the app.
How do I unsubscribe?
Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Unsubscribe. Your subscription will be cancelled and you'll be logged out automatically.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All notes are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. There is no cloud sync, no telemetry, and no advertising.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After subscribing and verifying your number once, the app works fully offline. Reminders are scheduled locally and fire without any internet connection.
What Android version do I need?
Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or above. The APK is under 20 MB and installs in seconds.
